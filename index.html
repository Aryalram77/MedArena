Pediatric dose calculator

Enter the child’s age and current weight, choose a medicine and match the concentration printed on the bottle.

Volume is shown per dose unless stated daily.
Safety: This is a basic educational calculator, not a prescription. Antibiotics must only be used when prescribed for a diagnosed infection; the correct medicine, dose and duration depend on the child and infection. Do not use for newborns/infants under 3 months, kidney or liver disease, allergies, or severe illness without clinician advice. Check the bottle concentration and measuring device. Do not combine products containing paracetamol.
Strengths documented in Nepal: paracetamol 125 mg/5 mL; levocetirizine 2.5 mg/5 mL; cefixime 50 or 100 mg/5 mL; amoxicillin 125 or 250 mg/5 mL; amoxicillin/clavulanate 200/28.5 or 400/57 mg per 5 mL. A 125/31.25 mg per 5 mL amoxicillin/clavulanate product is also listed locally, but is excluded from this high-dose example because it has a different ratio. Always follow the exact bottle label.

Levocetirizine doses are age-based label doses, not WHO recommendations. The amoxicillin and amoxicillin/clavulanate values shown are the WHO acute bacterial sinusitis example only (80–90 mg/kg/day of amoxicillin, divided every 12 hours); WHO notes antibiotics are unnecessary in most sinusitis cases. Cefixime is shown as 8 mg/kg/day for children aged 6 months and older, maximum 400 mg/day.
Sources: WHO AWaRe antibiotic book · WHO pediatric antibiotic dosing table · Cefixime label (age and maximum) · Levocetirizine label · Nepal National List of Essential Medicines · Nepal levocetirizine syrup profile · Nepal hospital pharmacy list
